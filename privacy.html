Privacy Policy

Paisa — expense tracker · Last updated 7 October 2026

The short version. Your expenses are stored in a Google Sheet inside your own Google Drive. They are not sent to us, not copied to our servers, and we have no way to read them. If you sign up with an email address, we store that address and a scrambled version of your password — nothing else.

Who runs this app

Paisa is an independent app built and maintained by Akshat Sinha. You can reach me at ak0005sinha@gmail.com with any question about this policy or your data.

How we use Google services

When you connect Google Drive, Paisa asks for a single permission: https://www.googleapis.com/auth/drive.file.

This permission is deliberately the narrowest one Google offers for Drive. It grants access only to files the app itself creates — in practice, the one spreadsheet named "Paisa — Expense Tracker", plus temporary files used to read a receipt. The app cannot see, open, or list any other file in your Drive. We do not request access to your documents, photos, or anything else.

We also receive your email address through Google sign-in, so the app can show you which account you are signed in as. For Google sign-ins this address stays on your device and is not sent to us.

Paisa's use of information received from Google APIs follows the Google API Services User Data Policy, including its Limited Use requirements.

What we do not do

  • We do not sell, rent, or share your information with anyone.
  • We do not show advertising, and we do not run advertising trackers.
  • We do not use analytics tools that profile you.
  • We do not use your data to train machine learning models.
  • We do not read, aggregate, or analyse your spending. We cannot — it is not stored anywhere we can reach.

Receipt scanning, explained

When you scan a payment screenshot, the image is uploaded to your own Google Drive and converted to a Google Doc, which is how Google extracts the text. The app reads that text back, deletes the temporary document, and works out the amount and merchant on your phone. The image never reaches us, and the temporary file is removed as soon as the text has been read.

Your control over your data

  • See everything. Open the spreadsheet in your Google Drive at any time. It is an ordinary file that belongs to you.
  • Delete everything. Delete that spreadsheet. The data is gone, because that file is the only copy.
  • Revoke our access. Visit your Google account permissions and remove Paisa. The app immediately loses all access; your spreadsheet stays with you.
  • Remove your email account. Email me and I will delete your email address and password hash from our records.
  • Clear your phone. Logging out removes the local copy held on your device.

Keeping it secure

All communication with Google happens over encrypted connections. Passwords are hashed on your device before being sent, using PBKDF2, and are hashed again before storage — so the original password is never transmitted and cannot be recovered from what we hold. Your 4-digit PIN never leaves your phone.

No system is perfectly secure. The structural protection here is that we hold almost nothing: there is no database of expenses to breach, because the expenses live in your Google account rather than ours.

Children

Paisa is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect information from them.
